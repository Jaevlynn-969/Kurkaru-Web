About Kurkaru

A little introduction to the fox behind the story.

Kurkaru is a soft-spoken arctic fox with a quiet hint of cunning in his eyes. In his heart lives a dream to become a writer like Fyodor Dostoevsky.

One day, he hopes to bring his own furry visual novel to life — a world shaped by imagination, emotions, and stories.
