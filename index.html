About Kurkaru

Who is behind the name?

Kurkaru is a soft-spoken arctic fox with a quiet hint of cunning in his eyes. In his heart lives a dream: to become a writer like Fyodor Dostoevsky, weaving stories that linger in the soul.

One day, he hopes to bring his own furry visual novel to life — a world shaped by imagination and feeling. He spends his days wandering between books, music, games, and the endless curiosity of learning new languages.
