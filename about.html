About Kurkaru

Beyond the name, the artwork, and the stories — this is a small look into the character behind the world of Kurkaru.

Kurkaru Lyco Dostoevsky

Arctic Fox · Writer · Dreamer · Gamer

Kurkaru is a soft-spoken arctic fox who spends his time wandering between books, music, games, and stories. Beneath his calm personality is a curious mind that is always looking for something new to learn and experience.

Species Arctic Fox
Role Writer / Creator
Languages Indonesian · English · German
Favorite World Books, Games & Music

Personality ✦

Quiet at first, but surprisingly expressive once he becomes comfortable with someone. Kurkaru is curious, imaginative, and enjoys conversations about stories, characters, music, games, and strange little ideas that appear out of nowhere.


He tends to observe before speaking. Sometimes that quietness can make him look mysterious, but there is usually a lot happening inside his head.

Things He Likes

  • Reading novels and visual novels
  • Furry visual novels
  • Writing stories
  • Listening to and playing music
  • Exploring different languages
  • Playing games with friends

Books & Stories

Books are one of the places where Kurkaru feels most at home. The dream of becoming a writer is heavily inspired by the ability of literature to leave a feeling behind long after the final page.


His biggest creative ambition is to eventually create a furry visual novel of his own — combining writing, characters, music, and visual storytelling into one world.

Music & Games

Music is another important part of Kurkaru's world. He enjoys listening to different kinds of music and also finds joy in playing with a band.


Games are equally important. From competitive titles to relaxed adventures, games provide another way to explore different worlds and spend time with friends.

“

Somewhere between a page, a melody, and a game, there is always another story waiting to be found.

— Kurkaru

The Dream ✦

One day, Kurkaru wants to create his own furry visual novel. Not simply a game, but a world where characters have their own stories, music carries emotions, and every choice leaves something behind.


It is still a dream, and there is a long road ahead. But every story written, every song discovered, and every new thing learned is another small step toward that world.

Welcome to Kurkaru's little world 🦊
