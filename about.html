About Kurkaru

A little story about the arctic fox behind this website.

Kurkaru Lyco Dostoevsky

Kurkaru is a soft-spoken arctic fox with a quiet hint of cunning in his eyes. Beneath his calm personality lives a strong imagination and a love for stories.


He dreams of becoming a writer like Fyodor Dostoevsky and hopes to create his own furry visual novel someday, a world built from imagination, emotion, and meaningful stories.

His Story

Kurkaru spends his days wandering between books, music, games, and different languages. He enjoys discovering new stories and experiences, believing that every little moment can eventually become part of a story.


Music is another important part of his world. Playing instruments and being part of a band gives him another way to express himself. Every melody feels like another chapter waiting to be written.
